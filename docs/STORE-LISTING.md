## Full description (Play Store 4000 char max / App Store no hard limit)

Life Budget Simulator turns budgeting into a game you actually want to open.

Every expense you track earns XP and builds a streak. Budgets are envelopes, not spreadsheets:
see exactly how much room is left in Food, Transport, or Fun before you overspend, with a
segmented fill bar that tells you at a glance.

The Simulator is the point. Drag a lever (cut coffee runs, add a side hustle, move house) and
watch a full 12-month net-worth projection react in real time, plotted against your current
baseline. It's the fastest way to see whether a habit change is worth it before you commit to it.

Built to keep you honest:
- Envelopes with over-budget and limit-reached states you can't miss
- Weekly spend breakdown and category donut, tap to isolate a category
- Savings goals with progress and a completion ETA
- Achievement badges for streaks, discipline, and hitting targets
- Light and dark themes, reduced-motion support, full keyboard/screen-reader accessible controls

Sign in and your transactions, budgets, and goals sync to your account. Row-level security
means no one else can read or write your data, not even us. Delete your account and everything
tied to it at any time from Profile.

No ads. No selling your data. Every number you see is your real spending, not a demo.
